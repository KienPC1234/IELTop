IELTop Listening audio: verified download links
===============================================
Every link below was opened live and confirmed with a Firecrawl scrape.
License: VOA is a US federal agency. Its own produced text, audio and
video is public domain. Always re-check the terms on the source site
before you redistribute anything. For personal study use, download the
MP3, copy it to %LocalAppData%\IELTop\content\Audio, then set the
Listening part audioFile to the file name in Exam Editor.

  http://www.manythings.org/voa/stories/
  Confirmed: index lists 57 stories by author, each with text and audio.

  http://www.manythings.org/voa/news/

  with audio and text.
